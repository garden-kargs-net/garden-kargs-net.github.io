We saw a big ball in the sky this morning.  Anna says, “Mom, look at that big ball in the sky”.  It was indeed very large.  It was the moon, we tried to get a good picture(just to be clear, Joshua took the picture).  I was driving in heavy traffic.  It was very beautiful just over the trees.

We also spent some time at the park today.  We have a very nice neighborhood park that is quiet and on a culdesac street.  We collected leaves and made some leaf rubbings with paper and crayons.  They turned out absolutely beautiful.  We met some new friends there too!  The kids loved playing with the kids and exploring nature.  The park is very shady and has a woodsy feel, as does our neighborhood here.
