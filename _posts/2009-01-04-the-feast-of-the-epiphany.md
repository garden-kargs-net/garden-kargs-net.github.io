#### Then they opened their coffers and presented Him with gifts of gold, frankincense, and myrrh.” –Matthew 2:11

Epiphany is Greek, meaning “to show” or “to manifest”.  This feast day we celebrate the 12th day of Christmas.  We remember this as the day that the wise men visited Jesus.
