Today Christopher came up to me and hugged me tightly.  He doesn’t do that very often, so it was very special to me and to him.  He wrapped his little arms around my neck and hugged for a long time.  It is in these moments that I realize what special blessings that God has given to me.   I am very thankful!  I find that when I talk with God throughout the day, my day goes much more smoothly.  If it is only a “thank you” that I give to him.  I know that he wants us to talk with him early and often.

We went to the pool today and played for some time.  The kids love going there.  Christopher came up to me and said, “eat” after about an hour.  We went in to have some dinner.  The pool always seems to drain them, so they are always hungry after playing.  There is a light haze over the mountains today.  It  is a pleasant day with the Pacific wind blowing  in making it very comfortable.

The hummingbird has visited more times today than I can count.  There are now 3 birds fighting for territory over the feeder.   I love to watch them go about their day.  It seems that most of it is spent eating rather drinking from the feeder.  I wonder what other things that they concern themselves with.  I’ll have to do some research.
