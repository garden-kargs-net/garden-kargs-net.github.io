When does fall begin for you? It starts when I walk out the door in the mornings to a cool crisp feeling in the air!  Also, when I see pumpkins on store shelves.

What is your favorite aspect of fall? I love all the beautiful colors, the cool crisp air, the smell of wood smoke, the leaves rustling…

What is your favorite fall memory? Our trip to the mountains of Tennessee several years ago.  I love shopping at all the shops, seeing the quilts, apple themed items, feeling the cool crisp air!

What do you like to drink in the fall?  I love the spiced apple cider!

What’s your favorite fall food? I have to say pumpkin is my favorite.  I make a pumpkin bread every fall that is sooooooooo good!  Recipe to follow.

What is fall weather like where you live? Very mild, but cool in the mornings.

What color is fall? Reds, browns, golden, yellows, greens(but not bright, just an olive green), oh and how can I forget orange!

What does fall smell like? Woodsmoke, apples and cinnamon.

Holiday shopping in fall: yes or no? Yes! I love to visit the craft fairs, fall festivals, and antique shops.  I love to look at fall decor.

If you could go anywhere in the fall, where would you go? New England.

**Easy Yummy Pumpkin Bread**

**1 tsp. baking soda  

1/2 cup hot water  

3/4 cup vegetable oil  

3 cups sugar  

4 eggs, well beaten  

2 cups pumpkin (I use one regular size can)  

1/2 tsp. cloves  

1 tsp. cinnamon  

3 1/2 cups all purpose flour  

1 tsp. salt**

**Dissolve baking soda in hot water and set aside. Mix oil, sugar, eggs,  

and pumpkin well. Add water with the soda and stir. Add the sifted dry  

ingredients. Divide into 2 loaf pans (greased and floured). Bake for  

50-60 minutes at 350.**

**Great served with cream cheese spread.**

Recipe from my friend, Judy.

Enjoy!!!
