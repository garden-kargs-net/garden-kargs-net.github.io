**It is a beautiful day today…filled with God’s goodness. We are thankful for the nice weather! We are thinking of and praying for all the families affected by Friday’s storms. We have a busy week planned for “Surrounded by Grace”. Our lenten mission is four evenings this week. We are also planning to attend a performance of “Peter and the Wolf” at the Children’s theatre! It is always fun watching the children perform. Spring is in the air. I can see the buds of the azaleas popping open into color. Thank you God for all things living!**  

** Thought for the day:**  

** “When we look at the cross, we realize how He loved us–only love could have made Him do that. When we look at the tabernacle, we know how He loves us now. He loves you and He loves me. He made Himself Bread of Life to satisfy our hunger for His love.”**  

** ~~Mother Teresa of Calcutta**
