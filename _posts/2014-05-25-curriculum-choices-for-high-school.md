It is that time!  I cannot believe that my daughter will be in high school.  I find that choosing books for this age is a bit more difficult.  After searching through many Catholic curriculum choices, I chose this [one](http://www.amazon.com/Introduction-Catholicism-A-Complete-Course/dp/1936045613/ref=sr_1_4?ie=UTF8&qid=1401075897&sr=8-4&keywords=didache+series) for religion:

# Introduction to Catholicism: A Complete Course

![](http://ecx.images-amazon.com/images/I/51H670b3WjL._SY344_BO1,204,203,200_.jpg)

It is a part of the [Didache](http://www.catholiceducation.org/articles/education/ed0187.html) series of religion books.

[Amazon](http://www.amazon.com/Introduction-Catholicism-A-Complete-Course/dp/1936045613/ref=sr_1_4?ie=UTF8&qid=1401075897&sr=8-4&keywords=didache+series) says that: *[Introduction to Catholicism, 2nd Edition](http://www.amazon.com/Introduction-Catholicism-A-Complete-Course/dp/1936045613/ref=sr_1_4?ie=UTF8&qid=1401075897&sr=8-4&keywords=didache+series), contains the same Catholic teaching found in the popular original textbook, with expanded topics. Based on the Catechism of the Catholic Church, this text covers the basic tenets of the Faith and what it means to be Catholic; to include an additional 200+ pages, a new design layout, and additional pieces of full-color liturgical art. Topics include: The Holy Catholic Church The Blessed Trinity The Paschal Mystery The Blessed Virgin Mary The Sacraments and Prayer Christian Morality and the Universal Call to Holiness New chapter on Social Doctrine This textbook is published with ecclesiastical approval from the Archdiocese of Chicago.*
