Happy birds… I heard them outside my window this morning.  This is the first time I’ve heard them here.  There are many trees lining the apartment complex, but I haven’t seen many birds at all.  They were playing on the eaves of the roof this morning, singing joyfully their morning song!  What a gift!

[![Christopher doing beads](http://garden.kargs.net/wp-content/uploads/thumbs/dscn3809_1.jpg)](http://garden.kargs.net/wp-content/uploads/dscn3809_1.jpg)

We got all of our book work done today.  Joshua gave us all a lesson on “[The Story of Money”](http://www.ny.frb.org/publications/result.cfm?comics=1).  We ordered these comic book like pamphlets from  the Federal Reserve Bank of New York.  And they are free!  Christopher is busy playing with play-doh and trains, hopefully not together!  He likes to make tracks with the trains in the play-doh sometimes.  And for music and inside P.E. today, we listened to “[Here we go Loopty Loo](http://www.amazon.com/Here-We-Go-Loopty-Loo/dp/B00004RDLF/ref=pd_bbs_sr_1?ie=UTF8&s=music&qid=1200338726&sr=8-1)” and songs to make you move!  We highly recommend!

I am making “[Sticky Chicken](http://busycooks.about.com/od/chickenbreastrecipes/r/stickychicken.htm)” in the crock-pot.  Hopefully it will be yummy!  It sure smells good!   This afternoon we need to get organized.  I love to read books by[Emilie Barnes](http://www.emiliebarnes.com/).  She gives me a lot of inspiration to be better organized.  Hopefully I will start to organize my space better.  She has books on creating beauty and warmth in our homes.  I just got this book,  “[Home Warming](http://www.amazon.com/Home-Warming-Secrets-Making-Welcoming/dp/0736908633/ref=pd_bbs_sr_1?ie=UTF8&s=books&qid=1200336448&sr=8-1)” in the mail last week.  I can’t wait to get started.  Oh, and speaking of home warming, check out [Kristen’s post on “A Little Steamy Spice for Winter”.](http://smalltreasuresinorangecounty.blogspot.com)

I was just reading [Dawn’s blog](http://dawnathome.typepad.com) today and saw the beautiful picture of the snow that is falling up where she lives.   What a gorgeous picture.   Speaking of inspiration, I am very inspired by the words that she writes and the memories that are being made in her home.
