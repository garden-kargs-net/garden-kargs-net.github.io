For all of us….

***Creation **  

It cost God but a thought,  

And, lo! the stars were wrought.  

He dropped them, spark on spark,  

Into the primal dark.*

It costs God but a thought,  

And, lo! a soul is wrought.  

A soul excelleth far  

The beauty of a star.  

–William V. Doyle, S.J.
