Today was a rainy day, so we stayed inside.  Anna worked on “tallying” in math.  She did really well.  We worked on some worksheets.  Joshua studied about our amazing brain and how it works.  We also have vocabulary words from that unit to learn.  Christopher did not feel well today.  He rested on the sofa for much of the day.

[![dscn3143.jpg](http://garden.kargs.net/wp-content/uploads/thumbs/dscn3143.jpg)](http://garden.kargs.net/wp-content/uploads/dscn3143.jpg)

As I was preparing dinner, Christopher, getting a second wind from the Motrin that I gave him earlier, came into the kitchen and wanted to “help”.  I was busy making some [chicken](http://garden.kargs.net/wp-content/uploads/2007/12/greatchickenrecipe.odt) in the crockpot, with rice.  For dessert, I made a [pumpkin roll](http://garden.kargs.net/wp-content/uploads/2007/12/pumpkinrollrecipe.odt).  There was still flour on the counter from our preparation, so Christopher took an interest in it, making tracks and little mounds with his hands and the cookie cutters that were nearby.  I was just amazed at how something so simple entertained him for at least an hour.  He kept coming back to it time and time again to play with it.

Still praying for direction in choosing our new home…
