I recently found this[**site**](http://missionbell.homestead.com/novenaforautisticchildren.html)** **for a novena prayed for those affected by autism.  This site was encouraging to me as I have a child with high functioning autism.  Our days can be very challenging together.  So, knowing that my child is being prayed for along with hundreds of others is very comforting to me.

Every child is created by God and should be treated with the utmost respect and love.  I believe my journey is teaching me more in this life than I could have ever possibly imagined.  The fact that my child has autism is seen as a disability by some and is in some respect, but he also has so many other abilities that are so awesome.  I can’t even begin to describe how wonderful this child is.  He has the ability to multi task like no one I have ever met.  So, we choose to focus on his **A**bilities rather than his** D**isability.  I know that he has a wonderful promising future.  We just need to help him along the way.

So, help us pray the novena for these special children and the ones who so lovingly journey with them daily!
