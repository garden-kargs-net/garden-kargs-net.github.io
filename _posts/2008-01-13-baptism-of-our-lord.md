Today we celebrate the baptism of our Lord.  Reminding us of our own baptism, whether it was shortly after we were born or as adults.  It was a new beginning.  Tomorrow we begin “ordinary” time in the Church.  Perhaps we can make this time extraordinary by doing our best to serve and love the One who gives us life and all that we have.  Just a few thoughts…

[![kids at zoo](http://garden.kargs.net/wp-content/uploads/thumbs/img_9062.jpg)](http://garden.kargs.net/wp-content/uploads/img_9062.jpg)

Yesterday we explored the local zoo.  The kids had a great time exploring.  Kids do that so well, investigating every thing in sight.  There was a nature trail that we explored.  It had many interesting things to touch and see.  We saw the animals.  The kids fed the swan and the fish.

In the mornings, Christopher always come to snuggle with me before I get out of bed.  Lately, he comes in and says, “Mornin, Mom”, then starts to tell me, “I dream”.  I always ask him what he dreams about, or “what he thinks about”while he is sleeping.  He often says “Anna” which comes out “Nana”.  He loves playing with his big sister.  He also thinks a lot about trains.  He absolutely loves trains and playing with his tracks.

Well, off to church now….
