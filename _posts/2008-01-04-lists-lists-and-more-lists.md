Today I tried to organize our school.  As I said before, organization is probably my weakness.  I know to be successful, I have to be organized.  It is essential especially for homeschoolers.  So, today I put up a bulletin board in our dining/learning area with a learning schedule, *chore*, oops I mean [“helper” charts](http://www.dltk-cards.com/chart)for each of my children and a church calendar complete with all of the Holy days of the church.  I had the idea to make the charts “helper” charts so that it sounded a little more like the kids were being helpers.  That is all it is really, if you think about it.  So, here is the learning schedule:

Monday, Wednesday, Friday———– we do this:

Math, Spelling, Handwriting, Reading, and History

Tuesday and Thursday————-we do this:

Math, Science, English, Art, and Religion

Wednesday is our library day.  We also do a Latin lesson on this day.

We primarily use [Catholic Heritage Curricula](http://www.chcweb.com/catalog/index.html) for our curriculum.  I also get Reading Rainbow DVD’s  from the local library.  The kids really enjoy watching those together.  I use [“Teach your child to read in 100 easy lessons”](http://www.amazon.com/Teach-Your-Child-Read-Lessons/dp/0671631985/ref=pd_bbs_sr_1?ie=UTF8&s=books&qid=1199476496&sr=8-1)with my second grader.  We are also using [“Hooked on Phonics”](http://www.amazon.com/Learn-Read-First-Grade-System/dp/1601438737/ref=pd_bbs_3?ie=UTF8&s=books&qid=1199477020&sr=8-3).  For my seventh grader, I supplement with [“Comic Strip Grammar”](http://www.amazon.com/Comic-Strip-Grammar-Grades-4-8-Greenberg/dp/0439086817/ref=pd_bbs_sr_1?ie=UTF8&s=books&qid=1199476537&sr=8-1). It makes grammar a little more interesting and funny.  We use [“Handwriting without tears”](http://www.amazon.com/Cursive-Handwriting-Jan-Olsen/dp/189162704X/ref=pd_bbs_4?ie=UTF8&s=books&qid=1199476840&sr=8-4) for both my 2nd and 7th graders.  For Latin, we are using[“Prima Latina”](http://www.amazon.com/Latina-Christiana-I-Set/dp/1930953003/ref=pd_bbs_2?ie=UTF8&s=books&qid=1199476904&sr=8-2).

I have a tentative schedule for cleaning.  I know I will add to it.  There is always more to do.  But here is the tentative schedule that I set up:

Monday—Laundry, including sheets, vacuum

Tuesday—Clean bathrooms

Wednesday—Tidy bedrooms, dust (*Chase and hopefully catch all of the little “dust bunnies”)  :)  

*

Thursday—Vacuum

Friday—Sweep, mop floors

The kitchen area is on a daily cleaning schedule.  We spend a lot of time there cooking and preparing meals.  I can’t think of any other areas at the moment.  I am always interested in getting ideas from other mothers though.  If I see any good ones, I’ll be sure to pass them on.

Until later…
