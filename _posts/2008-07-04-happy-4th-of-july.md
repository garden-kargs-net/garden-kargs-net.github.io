In other news, our tadpole is now a frog!  It seemed like it took forever.  We changed his habitat and researched what we are supposed to feed him now that he is a real frog!  It was fun watching it turn  from tadpole to frog!

We also now have a fish named “Polly”.  Her home is right next to the frog habitat on our nature shelf in the kitchen.  The kids love watching them.
