Today is the start of junior year for both my daughter(college) and my youngest son(high school).  It is so much different this year.  I miss them being little, but we have given them roots.  I pray that they will always remember their roots, and know that we are always here!  We are preparing for Chemistry, Religion 11, AP English literature, Algebra 2, Photography, and Government this year.  I am looking forward to learning along with him.  It is so comforting to have my children close.  Thank you Lord for always loving and protecting us!

**Prayers to Begin a School Year**

**God of wisdom and might,  
we praise you for the wonder of our being,  
for mind, body and spirit.  
Be with our children as they begin  
a new school year.  
Bless them and their teachers and staff.  
Give the strength and grace as their bodies grow;  
wisdom and knowledge to their minds  
as they search for understanding;  
and peace and zeal to their hearts.  
We ask this through Jesus Christ our Lord.  
Amen.**

*Prayer from “Catholic Household Blessings and Prayers”*
