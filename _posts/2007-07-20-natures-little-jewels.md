I’ve been doing some research trying to find out what species of hummers we have at our feeder.  I believe that we might have “Anna’s hummingbirds”.  It seems to match the description.  I found out that they are common here year round.

According to  http://www.hummingbirds.net/annas.html, ” Unlike most other                      hummers, Anna’s has a (minimal) song.”  I haven’t really heard alot of noise from them.  I also discovered from the same website about the plumage, ”                     Adult male: Metallic green back, dark rose-red crown and gorget,                      grayish breast.  

Adult female: Green back, grayish-white breast, white throat                      with some red spots, white tips on outer tail feathers.  

Juveniles: Young of both sexes look like the adult female,                      but may have unmarked throats.

[![Anna's hummer ?](http://garden.kargs.net/wp-content/uploads/thumbs/img_6298.jpg)](http://garden.kargs.net/wp-content/uploads/img_6298.jpg)

So, that will help me to identify them, maybe.  I also noticed a bigger bird drinking from the feeder.  It appears to be pale yellow with some brown. I am still trying to identify him.  A friend of ours said it could be an oriole.
