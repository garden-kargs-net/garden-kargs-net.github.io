Tuesday was our 14th wedding anniversary.  It doesn’t seem like 14 years, but I am thankful for my wonderful husband and for the family that we have created.  We went out to dinner, then to the beach to watch the sunset.  Unfortunately, we couldn’t really see the sunset for the cloud cover.  But, it was fun anyway.

The kids and I went to the library today.  Two of the neighbor friends went with us.  It was fun with all the kids.  I told Anna that she could get 7 books, because she is 7.  I was trying to limit the number because we still have more at home that we haven’t read yet.  Her friend, Kayla then asked what was my age.  She said, “Oh, you can get 40 because you’re 40.”

Kids say the funniest things, don’t they? I try to write down the cute things that my kids say and do.  I don’t want to forget those moments.  They pass so quickly as the kids are growing up everyday.

We also went to the pool today and had swimming lessons.  The instructor is very good with the kids.  She seems so calm and patient with them.
