**“Whatever is lovely… think on these things.”**

Phil. 4:8

- Surround yourself with only those things that make you smile.
- Light a candle.
- Buy some fresh flowers.
- Sit in the sunshine.
- Watch the birds.
- Try a new recipe.
- Bake some brownies.
- Hug your kids often.
- Call an old friend.
