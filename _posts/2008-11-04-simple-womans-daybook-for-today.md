*Outside My Window* … the sun is shining, making for a warm, pleasant day in the south.

***

*I am thinking* … about the election, of course!!

***

*I am thankful *… for my hard-working husband.

***

*From the kitchen* … I am thinking of making some [election day cupcakes](http://www.marthastewart.com/photogallery/election-day-party-foods?lnc=5a79cf380e1dd010VgnVCM1000005b09a00aRCRD&rsc=lpg_food&lpgStart=1&currentslide=14&currentChapter=3) for the family to enjoy…and still thinking about dinner…

***

*I am wearing* … a black short sleeve top and khaki pants.

***

*I am creating *…nothing at the moment.

***

*I am reading *… a book for my book club.

***

*I am hoping* …that our candidate wins today.

***

*I am hearing *… the washing machine spinning down with a fresh load of laundry, traffic passing by my house.

***

*Around the house* …tidy my desk, tidy the living room, planning to change linens and vacuum.

***

*One of my favorite things *…the beautiful colors of the leaves now.  There are reds, yellows and bright orange to brighten my day.

***

*A few plans for this week* …work on menu planning, start working on Christmas list, Thanksgiving plans, think about plans to organize closets.
