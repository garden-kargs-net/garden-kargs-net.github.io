As we prepare for a new day, many things remind me that we are truly surrounded by God’s grace.  I will just list a few.

—*The sounds of birds outside my window reminds me that He takes care of them, just as He takes care of us!*

*—My little boy’s strong arms around me this morning giving me a big hug, and his voice saying “Mornin, Mommy!*”

*—Knowing that He was watching over us all, even as we were sleeping*.

*—The beautiful colors and sounds of Spring gives our world beauty and peace.*

* —My little girl’s sweet voice, as she is hiding from her brother, whispering, “Help me, Mom”. *

*—Sweet giggles as they play hide and seek, and so early in the morning!! *
