Well, today Christopher was looking out the window in our kitchen and saw a cat in the woods behind our house.  I asked him, what kind of cat he saw?  He replied,  “a weal cat”.  A real cat it was, a tabby that I have seen before.  I don’t know where the cat lives, but seems to be skiddish, so we don’t ever get close enough to it.

Another funny thing that I recently heard was from Anna.  We were shopping for fabric to make curtains for her room.  She came up to me and said, “I think this one would be good for the bangs”.  

Well, I didn’t get it right away and she explained that the valance is like the “bangs” and the tiers are like the “hair”.  Makes sense when you think about it.

The funny things that our children say.  If I don’t write them down, I won’t remember…

Until later….

We’re off to the park on this beautiful day in our neighborhood.  It is in the 60’s outside! Just perfect for playing in the park!
