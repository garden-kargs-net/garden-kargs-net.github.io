Wow, it is 2009, as I think back over the past year, many thoughts come to mind.  The kids are growing so fast.  It doesn’t seem possible that my youngest is 4, almost 5.  He is growing by leaps and bounds, literally out of his pants.  I just noticed this morning while getting him dressed that his size 3T pants are a little short( he is a little fellow for his age).  I was driving down the road today thinking what will I do when they grow up and don’t need me as much anymore.  We put so much time and energy and love into growing these little ones right now.  I hope and pray they will all grow up knowing how much I enjoyed these special times with them.  This year, I need to do a better job at preserving those memories.  I do scrapbook and blog about our days, so I will continue to do just that!

So what happened last year?  It went by so quickly it seems.  Joshua turned 13, Anna turned 9 and Christopher turned 4.  I turned, well let’s just say a youthful 40ish, give or take!  My husband is 3 months older than me.  We moved into our new home, got settled in. (still unpacking boxes) does it ever end?  I can now park the van in half the garage YEAH!  There is still a lot of organizing to do!  We went on a wonderful anniversary/business trip to France and visited Lourdes, what a wonderful place!  I grew up in the Baptist church and came into the Church after meeting my husband.  I chose Bernadette as my confirmation name, so it was wonderful to visit the places where she was born, went to school, and the woods where she once played as a child.  So this trip was extra special for me!  Thanks to my wonderful husband for inviting me to go along.  Thanks to my inlaws for watching our kids!

I am thankful for another year.  I don’t like to make resolutions because I am usually not very successful with them.  But, I do hope to exercise more often, not just to lose weight, but to feel better, have more energy, and if weight loss comes out of it, GREAT!  If not, then at least I feel better and have the energy that I need to do all that I need to do each day!

I am thankful for another year to spend with my wonderful husband and kids!  I hope to be the wife and mother that they so truly deserve in the coming year!
