It has been awhile since I’ve posted.  A lot has happened since April.  We are now in San Marcos California on a 6 month job assignment with the new company that Steve is working with.  The area is beautiful with a lot of green trees, colorful flowers, tropical feel.  The weather is great! We are in a condo with a pool and spa.  The kids and I are enjoying exploring the area and swimming.  The beach is close.  We go there sometimes just to watch the surfers and to appreciate the beauty.  The ocean reminds me of how large and vast God’s love is for us.

*I pray that you … may have power, together with all the saints, to grasp how wide and long  

and high and  deep is the love of Christ (Ephesians 3:17-19).*
