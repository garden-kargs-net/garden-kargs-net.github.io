I get a lot of recipes online, but many come from the cookbooks that I have.  I made a great chocolate pie two days ago.  It came from the Karg family cookbook.  The recipe is for German Chocolate Pie:

*German Chocolate Pie  

compliments of Julie Lencer*

*2 unbaked pie crusts  

3 c. sugar  

7 Tbsp. cocoa  

1 tsp. vanilla  

1/2 c. butter  

1/2 tsp. salt  

4 eggs, beaten till foamy  

1 can milk  

2 c. coconut  

2 c. walnuts*

*Mix sugar, salt, and cocoa.  Add well beaten eggs.  Stir in vanilla, milk, and butter.  Add coconut and walnuts.  Pour into two 9 in. unbaked pie shells.  After baking, may be frozen to keep.  Bake 40-60 minutes at 350.*

*Note:  I omitted the walnuts, and used an average size can of evaporated milk(not the small one).  I also used the mixer after adding the eggs.  The result is that my pies turned out to be runny in the middle.  So, next time, I will use a little less milk, and not use the mixer.  But they tasted really yummy!*
