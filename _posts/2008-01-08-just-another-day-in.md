The kids like the stars that are on their helper charts when they earn one.

I found a wonderful book at our local library.  The name of it is [“The Burgess Animal Book for Children”](http://www.amazon.com/Burgess-Animal-Children-Yesterdays-Classics/dp/1599151715/ref=pd_bbs_sr_2?ie=UTF8&s=books&qid=1199831383&sr=8-2). Today we completed our art lesson.  The kids enjoyed learning about blending colors to make other colors.  We practiced using colored pencils to make bold and vibrant outlines of drawings.

I made apple crisp for dinner.  Yum! Hope it is well received by my family!

Until later…

** Apple Crisp Recipe**

From our Karg Family Cookbook  

Arlene Karg

*7 c. sliced and peeled apples  

1/2 c. white sugar  

1 1/2 Tbsp. Lemon juice  

1/3 c. flour  

1 c. uncooked oats (quick or not)  

1/2 c. brown sugar  

1/2 tsp. Salt  

1 tsp. Cinnamon  

1/3 c. melted margarine*

*Arrange apples in a 9×12 pan.  Do not grease the pan.  Sprinkle with lemon juice and white sugar.  Combine all other dry ingredients and add melted margarine. Mix till crumbly and sprinkle on apples. Double or triple topping only. Bake at 350 to 375 for 30 to 40 minutes. *
