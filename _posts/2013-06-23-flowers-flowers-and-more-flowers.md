flower is the Columbine, a beautiful pale blue to white, 3 inches long and bell shaped composed of 5 petals. We saw some blooming at the nature center. The name comes from the Latin word *colum*, for “dove”. The flower pedals resemble a group of birds in flight! How lovely is that? The columbine is important as a nectar source for hummingbirds and is a protected flower, so no picking them! :)
