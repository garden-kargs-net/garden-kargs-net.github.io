Ok, I know that today wasn’t on our schedule as a park day, but the weather was just *too* beautiful to stay inside.  We had a weekly library trip, then headed to the park to play.  No complaints from my family about that!  We played at the park for about an hour.  My kids made some new friends, many friendly faces all around.

Well, the apple crisp went over really well with everyone!  There wasn’t much left after dinner.  We got some new books today at the library.  The kids seem to enjoy the Magic School Bus DVD’S.  So, we borrowed some of those too.

Tonight, we went to the church to “practice” for first reconciliation for Anna.  The program was beautiful.  We made a forgiveness candle with colored paper and a glass candle holder, with liquid starch to make the colored paper stick.  It looked really beautiful!

We practiced a really beautiful song:
