Today was a beautiful sunny day in the southeast! So, we went to the backyard for some much needed exploring! Christopher found some bugs underneath the leaves. We always called them roly polys as kids, but they are actually called “pill bugs”. He was asking a lot of questions about this little “tickle” bug as he called it. He put it on his hand and let it crawl all over! I found this [site](http://www.pestworldforkids.org/pillbugs.html) to help better understand this little “tickle” bug!
