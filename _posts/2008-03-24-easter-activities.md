This past weekend, we visited with family members and had an Easter egg hunt at my sister’s house.  The kids had a great time seeing their cousins and playing.  We went to Mass yesterday morning.  The church was decorated so beautifully, celebrating our risen Lord.  I had a cranky little one, so much of my time was spent in the foyer.  That is how it goes sometimes.  We came  home and relaxed for the rest of the afternoon and evening.

[![250px_Antarctica_Map.png](http://garden.kargs.net/wp-content/uploads/thumbs/250px_Antarctica_Map.png)](http://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Atlas_of_Antarctica)

[![392px_Antarctic__gentoo_penguin__js__60.jpg](http://garden.kargs.net/wp-content/uploads/thumbs/392px_Antarctic__gentoo_penguin__js__60.jpg)](http://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Image:Antarctic%2C_adelie_penguin_%28js%29_57.jpg)

Today we are studying about Antarctica.  We made salt maps of the continent of Antarctica and decorated them with paints.   The kids had a great time mixing the flour, salt and water together.   The recipe is three parts flour, one part salt, and enough water to make a paste.  We made mountain peaks for the Transantarctic mountains that separate the western and eastern regions of Antarctica.   The kids put snow peaks on the mountains.

We continue our bird watching and our journal of how many we see.  I did find out the name of the bird that we could not identify.  It is the pine warbler.  This is a photo that I found of him…

[![800px_Dendroica_pinus.jpg](http://garden.kargs.net/wp-content/uploads/thumbs/800px_Dendroica_pinus.jpg)](http://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Image:Dendroica_pinus.jpg)

  They are beautiful birds and travel in numbers.  They like to share the feeder with their own kind, but squabble a bit when another kind of bird appears.  We continue to see the cardinals in all of their red glory.  They are really beautiful birds.  We saw a red tail hawk in our wooded backyard the other day.  We see woodpeckers and mourning doves at the feeders lately.  I still haven’t found my camera.   I will take more pictures when I find it!

For dinner we are having chicken in the crockpot.  I just put some drumsticks in with some chicken with rice soup, some seasonings and some curry.  Hope it turns out yummy!

Until later…
