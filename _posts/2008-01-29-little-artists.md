One of my favorite memories of my dear grandmother is when she taught me to do things.  I still remember many of her lessons.  They weren’t usually planned, just as we were going about our day.  I spent a lot of time with her growing up.

[![Anna's french braid](http://garden.kargs.net/wp-content/uploads/thumbs/img_9333.jpg)](http://garden.kargs.net/wp-content/uploads/img_9333.jpg)

One of the things that she taught me was how to make a french braid.  I still remember vividly the day that she taught me. So, tonight I made a french braid in Anna’s hair.  We’ve done it many times before, and as always I remember my dear grandmother and the many lessons that she taught me with her love!

Today we did watercolor painting.  Christopher is fascinated by all of the wonderful shapes he can make with water, paint, and a paint brush!  He created quite the masterpiece!  Joshua and Anna also created their own scenes using this wonderful paint that I found at our local grocery store, of all places.  The paint that we used is called [Prang Watercolors.](http://www.amazon.com/PRANG-OVAL-8-WATER-COLORS/dp/B000AN0VYW/ref=pd_bbs_sr_5?ie=UTF8&s=electronics&qid=1201654623&sr=8-5) They are very vibrant and  flow very well on the paper.   Christopher used another brand.  He likes to mix the paint colors, which drives the older two crazy!  Anyway this is what I saw when they were finished:
