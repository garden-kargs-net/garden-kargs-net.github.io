We are bird watchers.  We love to see and listen to the birds in our backyard.  Our backyard has a canopy of trees and bushes to attract all kinds of wildlife.  I found a dvd at the library.  We borrowed it last week.  The name of it is “[Backyard Habitat](http://www.amazon.com/Best-Backyard-Habitat/dp/B00125WAUU/ref=pd_bbs_sr_1?ie=UTF8&s=dvd&qid=1209663736&sr=8-1)“.  The kids are watching it today as a part of a science lesson.  We’ve seen many different birds lately, among them a red tail hawk, a possum, and many squirrels.  We have a new beautiful birdfeeder now, thanks to my wonderful mother in law and father in law!   We really like  it a lot!  So do the birds!

We’ve had a rough morning with our little guy, Christopher.  He is having  a not so good day.  I guess we all have bad days sometimes!  It reminds me of the inspiration that I got from a motivation site.   I choose my battles carefully.  We will survive this challenging time!
