Ok, so tonight, I heard Anna and Christopher talking.  Anna says, “Chris, I want to play alone.”  Christopher replied,  “No, I want to play ALONE with you!”  She just looked at him and was thinking… Now what do I say?   What other line can I give him so that he will stop following me around?  At least, she was nice about it.  Oh, the things that kids say.

We are getting it unpacked one box at a time.  We got a lot accomplished this weekend.  Another load is coming tomorrow from storage units.  How on earth did we accumulate so much stuff?  Oh well, we are donating a lot of it.  It feels so good to get rid of clutter!

Until later…
