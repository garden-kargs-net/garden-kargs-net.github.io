I was reading today about how clutter is often called “physical noise”.  If you think about it, this is so true.  The clutter tends to clutter our minds.  I got some inspiration from a website that I found today.  I have looked at this one before, just never took it very seriously, until now…

I really need help with organizing my home and all of the “stuff” that we have.  I function much better with less, yet how did we accumulate all of this excess, stuff we never use and don’t need?

Well, I am on the road to a little, and hopefully a lot of organization in my life.  The website is from the Fly Lady:

[http://www.flylady.net/pages/begin_babysteps.asp](http://www.flylady.net/pages/begin_babysteps.asp )

I am beginning with the first baby step.  I will do my best to become better organized and find some peace in my surroundings.  Right now, all that I see is noise…
