Our new home, that is.  There are boxes everywhere, of course.  But, at least we now have a place to call home.   I really like the new house.  The neighborhood is nice.  The neighbors are nice.  It just feels like “home”.  What a good feeling!  We got our cat, “Rebel” back today.  A friend of ours kept her for us while we searched for the right place.  She is getting used to being in a new place too.  I think she likes it so far, though.

I’ve been down with the flu for the past few days.  Joshua got it first, then I came down with fever and chills on Friday morning.  Needless to say, I missed my scrapbooking weekend.  I was disappointed, but so it goes.  There will be other opportunities.

I woke up yesterday morning to see a woodpecker outside my bedroom window pecking on a tree in the front yard.  He was working hard for his breakfast, it seemed!

Until later…
