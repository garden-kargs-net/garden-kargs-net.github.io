**Remember those who:**

- don’t know how to navigate our world as freely as the rest of us…
- are bothered by lights, sound and everyday noise that we can filter out…
- struggle daily to live in a world that doesn’t understand…
- live a life most often being misunderstood…

- And, most importantly, remember:
- they bring joy to those of us who care for them…

**Thank you!  for being:**

- patient
- kind
- loving
- to my special child!
