We are trying to recover from coughs and colds.  It has been a very rough winter with sickness.  We welcome spring in a few days!  Our daffodils are peeking out, welcoming the warm days and sunny skies.  The birds are happy and singing.  Speaking of birds, we saw a cardinal, a tufted titmouse, and a downy woodpecker at the feeders today.  There is still a bird that I cannot identify yet.  We have some bird books that we are looking through to try and identify it.  It has a green body and head with black and white tail feathers.  I would take a picture, but misplaced my camera again!

Tuesday was Johnny Appleseed Day.  We discussed this story and made a yummy dessert for dinner.  I found the recipe at [allrecipes.com.](http://allrecipes.com/Recipe/Country-Apple-Dumplings/Detail.aspx) The name of the recipe is **Country Apple Dumplings**. It was so wonderful!

INGREDIENTS  

2 large Granny Smith apples, peeled and cored  

2 (10 ounce) cans refrigerated crescent roll dough  

1 cup butter  

1 1/2 cups white sugar  

1 teaspoon ground cinnamon  

1 (12 fluid ounce) can or bottle Mountain Dew

DIRECTIONS  

Preheat the oven to 350 degrees F (175 degrees C). Grease a 9×13 inch baking dish.  

Cut each apple into 8 wedges and set aside. Separate the crescent roll dough into triangles. Roll each apple wedge in crescent roll dough starting at the smallest end. Pinch to seal and place in the baking dish.  

Melt butter in a small saucepan and stir in the sugar and cinnamon. Pour over the apple dumplings. Pour Mountain Dewâ„¢ over the dumplings.  

Bake for 35 to 45 minutes in the preheated oven, or until golden brown.
