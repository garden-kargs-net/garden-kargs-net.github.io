Today a friend, my daughter and I went for a nice walk on the walk/bike trail. 

[![10402743_10204432839847798_3518207731733882394_n](http://garden.kargs.net/wp-content/uploads/2014/06/10402743_10204432839847798_3518207731733882394_n-300x168.jpg)](http://garden.kargs.net/wp-content/uploads/2014/06/10402743_10204432839847798_3518207731733882394_n.jpg)

 My daughter enjoyed biking, while my friend and I walked the path.  It was beautiful with a lot of trees and birds singing.  We even found a nest with baby birds underneath a bridge.

[![10367166_10204432831967601_5904357010746276731_n](http://garden.kargs.net/wp-content/uploads/2014/06/10367166_10204432831967601_5904357010746276731_n-300x168.jpg)](http://garden.kargs.net/wp-content/uploads/2014/06/10367166_10204432831967601_5904357010746276731_n.jpg)
