### On my birthday, Christopher told me, “Mommy, I got you something “noisy” for your birthday!” So, I said to him, Is it you? He just smiled and said, “no”. Then came in with this…. Aren’t they beautiful? It is a set of windchimes with a beautiful brass and beaded butterfly! I found a home for them today by my bedroom window. I said they are too beautiful to be outside!
