This morning we went to early children’s Mass.  It started at 8:30am.  We then went to PSR class.  After church, we went to lunch and stopped by the starving artist’s sale that is just down the road from where we live.  Joshua asked me, “Do we need to bring them some food”?  I explained to him what the term “starving artists” meant.  Only then did he understand.  We found some very nice canvas paintings at an affordable price.  Anna picked out an underwater fish one for her wall.  Joshua chose a very unusual one that had nice colors and looked like “birds”, maybe but I’m not quite sure what it is supposed to be.  We also chose a beach scene, one to remind us of our time in California and the beach.

We came home and were trying to catch up on some schoolroom organizing, when I remembered that I needed to test Joshua on some things that we did last week.  So, as I am trying to organize our shelves, Christopher found a puppet craft kit that we haven’t worked on yet.  So, I stopped to work on that with him.  Needless to say, the school books and pencils are still in no real order on the floor.  So, that is how my day goes sometimes.  I always start with good intentions to do something and I get sidetracked a million times, it seems.  But we did get the testing done!  Just not graded yet!!

Sometimes, it is so difficult to try and explain to people that I meet daily about homeschooling and how I do it?  Well, I have been thinking about that lately.  Just the other day, in the dentist’s office, the staff were commenting about how, “Wow, you have your hands full”.  I will have to say that I agree with them.  My only answer to their question about how I do it? is that I don’t.  I have someone much bigger that me that helps me daily.  Otherwise, I would not be able to do what I do everyday.  So, it is to Him that I give all the credit.

Until later…
