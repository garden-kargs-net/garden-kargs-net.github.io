Well, it did snow after all!  We got up this morning with no snow in sight.  Anna was so disappointed.  She hasn’t seen snow except when she was a baby, so she wanted to stay up all night and wait for it!  After about a couple of hours this morning, it started to snow.  The accumulation was mainly on the cars.  But, enough for the kids to make a snowman.

They made snowballs and had a snowball fight.  There just wasn’t enough snow to sled, but they were planning that too!
