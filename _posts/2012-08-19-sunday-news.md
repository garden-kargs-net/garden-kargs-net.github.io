Today we returned home from a weekend trip to Ocoee, TN. The boy scout troop went on a whitewater adventure, and the kids and I tagged along. It was a fun time. We had some excellent dinner prepared by the troop, and chili prepared by my son! He is quite the cook. My little one had fun building fire rings(no fire, yet!), just the rocks and sticks. We roasted marshmallows. Tent camping in the rain isn’t really for me, so I have to say that my daughter and I opted for the nearest inn, and there was room for us! Yay! We also had Mass tonight at our local parish. Thank you Lord for safe travel and good times with our friends and family! :)
