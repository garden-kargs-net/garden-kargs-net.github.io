I made Beef Stew in the crockpot today.  It was well received by my family.  So, I’ll share the very easy recipe:  

*Beef Stew*

*1 1/2 pounds of stew meat  

Nature’s seasoning  

4 organic carrots, sliced  

1/2 onion, sliced  

1 can tomatoes, sweet onion flavored or any flavor you want, or you can just use plain  

5-6 Tbsp. Worcestershire sauce*

*Cook all day, serve with rice or noodles.  We had rice with our dinner tonight!*
