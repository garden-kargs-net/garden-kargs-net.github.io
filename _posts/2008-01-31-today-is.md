[Feast of St. John Bosco](http://www.catholicculture.org/liturgicalyear/calendar/day.cfm).  He is the patron saint of schoolchildren, youth, and boys, among others.  It is said that he had a talent for juggling and performing tricks.  These two traits attracted children to him so that he could teach them the catechism.

Today we closed on our new home.  We will start packing today and move in on Monday.  We are very thankful to have a new home.

Today, Christopher told me, with words, “I love you too, Mom”.  This is very special because up until today, he has never been able to use his words to say that.  Sure, he hugs and kisses me a lot, but since we noticed his lack of language and speech, he hasn’t been able to express himself with words.  I am so very thankful that his words are now becoming more and more numerous and that he is finally able to communicate with words!

Since, that first “I love you”, he has been saying it nonstop!  Thank you God for little ones and for giving me the chance to hear those words!
