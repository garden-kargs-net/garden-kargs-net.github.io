We were having just an ordinary day, until…Anna and I saw a robin out the window.  I pointed her out to Anna.  We were ooing and awing over the robin.  I walked away when Anna said, “Mom, I see a turtle.”

Now, we have never seen turtles in our backyard before, so I thought, she sees something that looks *like* a turtle.  We went outside and discovered a large box turtle behind our snowball bush.  The excitement on Anna’s face was priceless as we checked him out.  We put him in an aquarium and gave him some oranges and lettuce.  He seemed content until Christopher decided to throw some leaves on him.

We had a discussion about what we will do with him and decided to donate him to the nature center that we will visit tomorrow.  I think he will be much happier there with all the other turtles.  Joshua decided that he needs a little hiding place while in the aquarium, so he built a box with holes and a peek hole.  Very clever!  I hope the turtle makes it until tomorrow…
