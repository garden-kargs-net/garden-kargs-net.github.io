A visiting priest at our church this morning reminded us all about what[Lent](http://www.aboutcatholics.com/worship/lent_catholic_church/) is all about…

Lent is not just about giving up that candy bar or coke… it is more about seeing a change in ourselves, in how we behave, in how we see others.  Seeing strangers as friends that you haven’t met yet, giving to others who are in need, letting our light shine in a dark world.  We can be assured that we are on the right path especially if we are struggling to do the right thing, being criticized by others, or things are just not perfect.  God wants a willing heart, He will lead us from there.

The Season Of Lent…

The 40 days before Easter, beginning with [Ash Wednesday.](http://catholicism.about.com/od/holydaysandholidays/p/Ash_Wednesday.htm)

The traditional colors worn during Lent are purple(a royal color) to prepare for the King.

Lent is a time for reflection, imitating Jesus’ withdrawal into the wilderness for 40 days.
