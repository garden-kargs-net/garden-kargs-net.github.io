Another new year, so much to plan and do.  I have to admit that I am lacking in the area of organization.  I am interested in what other homeschooling moms do to get themselves organized.  We have our school room in our dining room area now as we are still in an apt.  We are in the process of finding our new home.  I have two bookshelves to organize my room.  We also use the dining room table for our learning table.  I have each child’s books on a single shelf, with the other shelves used for crayons, pencils and reading books.  A little library shelf, as I call it.  I have a desk with two printers and our laptop.  It works for now.  There is only so much you can do in an apt.  But, we are making it work.  I miss watching the birds.  We are planning to make some bird feeders using pinecones, peanut butter, and seed soon.  Maybe they will come to our balcony so that we can watch them.

Brrr…it has been cold here the last few days.  We are waking up to single digit temps. in the mornings with wind chills even lower.  It does warm up during the day though.  I had forgotten how cold it can get here after spending 6 months in Southern California.  What a beautiful experience that was for our family!

Oh, I wanted to share this wonderful “recipe” from a delightful book that I am reading,

From [“Mitten Strings for God”](http://www.amazon.com/Mitten-Strings-God-Reflections-Mothers/dp/0446676934/ref=pd_bbs_2?ie=UTF8&s=books&qid=1199412937&sr=8-2)

**Recipe for Perserving Children**

*Ingredients:*

1 grass grown field

several dogs and puppies

pebbles and sand

1/2 dozen children or more

1 brook

*Method*:

Into field, pour children and dogs, allowing to mix well.  Pour brook over pebbles until slightly frothy.  When children are nicely brown, cool in warm bath.  When dry, serve with milk and freshly baked gingerbread.
