Today is Dec. 31, New Year’s Eve.  As I pause to reflect on the year, I think of it as a whirlwind, really.  We’ve moved, sold our house in GA, moved to two new cities.  We survived.  The kids are fine.  Our family is still intact, thankfully!  I will try to remember the positive things about this chapter of our lives, the wonderful new friends that we met, the chance to live in a beautiful area of the U.S.

[![Christopherwithnewbike](http://garden.kargs.net/wp-content/uploads/thumbs/dscn3645.jpg)](http://garden.kargs.net/wp-content/uploads/dscn3645.jpg)

These past few days have been a time for relaxing.  Steve is home with us.  We celebrated Christmas.  The kids were blessed with many toys and good wishes from family and friends.  Steve assembled Christopher’s new tricycle yesterday.  Christopher was so proud.  He had been looking at it for days as it remained in the box.  He would look at it, push the box around.  I must say he was very patient.  There were trains to play with, though.  That kept him busy.

[![Christmas2007atchurch](http://garden.kargs.net/wp-content/uploads/thumbs/dscn3651.jpg)](http://garden.kargs.net/wp-content/uploads/dscn3651.jpg)

We went to Mass last night.  The church was sooo beautiful with the flowers and the large wonderful tapestry of Mary and Jesus hanging above the altar.  The tree by the altar was decorated with ornaments that had significance of the season.  The choir sounded wonderful.  We are so blessed with our freedom to worship.

Well, tomorrow is the beginning of a New Year.  What resolutions will we make and keep!
