**Chicken Casserole**

*3 c. cooked chicken  

1 diced onion  

1 chopped green pepper  

1/2 stick margarine  

1 can cream of mushroom or celery soup  

1/2 c. chicken broth  

5 oz jar Kraft English Cheese  

1 can water chestnuts  

1 can mushrooms  

Cooked pasta(your choice) I use penne  

*

*Saute onion, green pepper in butter until reduced. Stir in chicken, soup and cheese.  Take off heat.  Sitr in water chestnuts, broth, and mushrooms.  Stir in cooked pasta.  I also add a dash of soy sauce for flavor.  Bake at 350 for 30-45 min. covered in a casserole dish! *

*Enjoy!!!!!!*
