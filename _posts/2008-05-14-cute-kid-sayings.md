When Christopher is trying to think of something lately, he points with his little finger to his head and says, “Tink, Tink, Tink.” Obviously meant to be “think, think, think”.  It is just the cutest thing that I’ve seen lately.  So, I had to share.

Today was library day, then to the park to play.  The kids had a great time.  I had a great time watching!  Bad weather is in the forecast for tomorrow, so we will see how that goes.

Oh, and today,  Anna asked me, “Was Jesus Catholic?”  Joshua was quick to answer her question, explaining that Jesus was Jewish.  This question just came out of the blue as we were getting into the van at the library, which is right across the street from our church.  She began with, “I’ve been wondering”.

Until later…
