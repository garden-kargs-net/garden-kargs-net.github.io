Christopher and Anna are doing so well with swimming lessons. Yesterday I noticed that Christopher was actually floating with his life vest on in the middle of the pool, all by himself.  He was actually swimming from the steps to the ladder. He was so proud of himself! He kept doing it over and over again!  He said, “Look Mommy, I wim, I wim”.  He had swim lessons today and did very well. Anna is learning to swim underwater. He is getting more brave each time.

The hummingbirds are chasing one another again today. There is one dominant bird that seems to chase the others away!
