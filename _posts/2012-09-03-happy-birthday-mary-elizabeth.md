**14 years ago, I said hello to my beautiful daughter, Mary Elizabeth…the next day, God called her to her heavenly home. I said goodbye for now, and went home without her. As long as I have breath, I will make sure that her name is spoken, and that she is remembered. She came into this world, and left way too soon. Many milestones have come and gone. Every year I think of what she would look**  

** like now…what kind of music she would enjoy…what would be her favorite food…pink or purple…I missed that first tooth, first word, first step…**  

** She was baptized…received last rites…this year would be the year that she would be confirmed. I miss having her here to experience these things with us. Her heavenly body is perfect…God made it that way the moment she left me to be with Him.**  

** She remains forever in my heart, and I will never forget… I know she is dancing just as I saw her little spirit dancing away the night she left me to be with Jesus…yes that really happened…for that I am very thankful! His heavenly presence was with me in my hospital room that night, just as He is with me today. Thanks for letting me share her life with you…Happy Birthday in heaven sweet angel. Mommy loves you more than you will ever know!  

**

**Yesterday, my family and I were attending Mass. During the “Our Father” prayer, I was standing on the end and had my hand outstretched while holding hands with my family. I felt her little hand slip into mine, and the tears started to flow. It was a sweet, sweet reminder that she is still with me. She will never truly leave! :)**
